Places I've Pooped 💩

A tongue-in-cheek iPhone app for dropping a pin everywhere you've, well, been. Long-press the map, give the stop a 1–5 rating and a #1 / #2 tag, and watch the map fill up with color-coded pins.

  • Rating-colored pins, plus Satellite, OpenStreetMap and Heatmap layers
  • Statistics with streaks, a ratings chart and distance from home
  • 23 achievement badges that unlock as you go
  • Timeline, countries & states visited, photos and video per pin
  • Offline maps, full backup / restore — everything stays on the phone

It's a native iOS app, so it can't run in the browser — tap the buttons under the phone to flip through real screens. Demo data, San Francisco edition.
